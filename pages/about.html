About Kamo. G

Music, identity and family legacy

Kamo. G is a Pretoria-born South African artist whose work draws on hip-hop, Afro-influenced sounds and personal storytelling.

Born in Pretoria and raised in Kempton Park, he grew up in a musical family where gospel, hip-hop, Afrobeat and kwaito were part of everyday life. His work is grounded in identity, family and the realities of South African life.

Son of Moses is an artistic identity and campaign concept rooted in family legacy. It honours his father, Moses, and frames a continuing story of purpose, growth and self-definition.
